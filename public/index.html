01 · About

Both sides of the table,
one set of values.

I'm a Rural and Suburban Mail Carrier (RSMC) and the President of CUPW Elliot Lake–Blind River Local 532, representing the members I work alongside every day.

I'm also the person the local calls when it needs something built: a database that turns raw route data into clear reports, a server that keeps our services online, a script that turns a spreadsheet full of anomalies into an argument management can't wave away.

I believe in evidence over rhetoric, in tools that outlive their author, and that health and safety is never a bargaining chip.

02 · What I do

Three hats, one mission.

Union leadership

Leading a small urban/rural local through difficult terrain — contract enforcement, grievances, health & safety advocacy, and asking the questions others are told not to.

03 · Selected work

Things I've built
(and stood behind).

Route Restructure Database

PythonData

A pipeline that ingests Canada Post route restructure exports (Access databases, workbooks, PDFs) and produces per-route HTML reports and analysis workbooks — used to verify claims, surface anomalies, and back members with facts.

Local 532 web infrastructure

DockerNPMplus

The front door for *.cupw532.ca: a Docker host running a hardened reverse proxy with automatic TLS, service-by-service isolation, and a disaster-recovery runbook good enough that a stranger could rebuild it from scratch.

Financial oversight tooling

SpreadsheetsGovernance

Tracking systems for local finances — outstanding balances, budgets, and reporting — turning "trust me" into "here's the ledger." Boring by design, indispensable in practice.

Advocacy writing

H&SCampaigns

Letters and briefs on route restructure impacts: winter darkness, overtime pressure, dock contention, safety on unlit roads. Written plainly, because a good argument shouldn't need jargon.
